Our leadership team is a small but diverse group with a wide range of backgrounds and experiences.

#### Jud Cole, VP, Software Engineering

As VP of Software Engineering, Jud has seen the software industry develop into a vital and thriving
market during over 30 years of personal computer programming experience. His repertoire of
programming languages includes Delphi, Assembler, C, C++, C#, Java, and countless others. He has
worked with clients of all sizes from all around the globe. This diverse knowledge has been the
basis for the development of an outstanding coding style that embraces the best of current theories
on effective development practices.

Jud has co-founded multiple successful companies over his career, including Blink, Inc., a company
to market Blinker, a high-performance linker and DOS Extender, and the original Code Healer Group to
market CodeHealer, a static source code analyzer.

Jud is a vital and inspired founder and leader for the company. His passion for programming is
contagious and regularly charges the team with dedication and inspiration to build and support
quality products that can help Code Healer Group's customers.

Read more about Jud on his [Portfolio website](https://www.judcole.com/).

#### Leah Flournoy, VP, Marketing

The VP of Marketing brings twenty years of experience developing and managing customer loyalty
solutions for businesses ranging from one man shops to Fortune 100 clients in a variety of
industries.

She joined Code Healer Group in 2002 to help bring CodeHealer to the world and has supported the
company in its ongoing growth. In 2010, she was pleased to announce the renaming of the company to
Code Healer Group. She says, "Changing our name to **Code Healer Group** was the most logical
branding choice. CodeHealer is a great product, and we are excited to carry the name into all we do."

Leah's customer-focused approach helps Code Healer Group address the needs of both corporations and
individual programmers. In the ongoing development of CodeHealer, programmers and managers are often
interviewed about how they work and what could help them the most. This helped make CodeHealer
intuitive and easy to use, while giving programmers the features that they need to help improve
source code quality.

#### John Oates, VP, European Operations

With forty-five years of computing experience behind him, the VP of European Operations has seen
computers evolve from valves to transistors to the massively integrated circuits of today. He has
managed programming shops as they progressed from working in machine code to using assemblers, and
all the way to using the amazingly powerful development tools that are available today.

He does, however, think that some things never change. Successful, cost-effective systems have
always required clear specifications and requirements, adequate training and standards, and rigorous
coding discipline.

John is delighted to be working with Jud again. More than twenty years ago, they worked together to
give the world Blinker, the salvation of many Clipper developers. Today John is working with Jud
and Code Healer Group to "take the glad tidings of CodeHealer and static code analysis to the
stressed developers of Europe";
